I am a graduate student in the Department of Artificial Intelligence at Chung-Ang University, Seoul, Korea, advised by Prof. Hwanhee Lee.

My research focuses on multilingualism and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), tackling language bias and cross-lingual inconsistency. I am also interested in LLM agents and question answering.

For more details, please check my CV or reach me via email: tom0365@cau.ac.kr
