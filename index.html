Jeonghyun Park

I am an M.S. student in the Department of Artificial Intelligence at Chung-Ang University (Seoul, Korea), advised by Prof. Hwanhee Lee in the Language Intelligence Lab (LILAB). My research centers on Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), with a particular emphasis on multilingual settings where language bias and cross-lingual inconsistency remain open challenges. I also explore ambiguity resolution in question answering — investigating how LLMs interpret and reason over semantically ambiguous queries that require multi-hop inference. Recently, I have been collaborating with Adobe Research on understanding code-switched text and developing benchmarks at the intersection of ambiguity interpretation and complex reasoning.
