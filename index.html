Jeonghyun Park M.S. @ Chung-Ang University

M.S. student in the Department of Artificial Intelligence at Chung-Ang University, advised by Prof. Hwanhee Lee at the Language Intelligence Lab (LILAB). My research centers on Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), with emphasis on multilingual settings where language bias and cross-lingual inconsistency remain open problems. I also study ambiguity resolution in question answering — how LLMs interpret and reason over semantically ambiguous queries that demand multi-hop inference. Currently collaborating with Adobe Research on code-switched text understanding.
